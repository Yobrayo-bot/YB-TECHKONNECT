### 1\. THE VISION AND PURPOSE

### 1.1 What exactly is YB TECHKONNECT?

YB TECHKONNECT is a digital marketplace that connects individuals, businesses and organisations experiencing technical problems with qualified service providers who can solve those problems.

The platform acts as an intermediary between the person requiring a service and the person providing it.

For example:

A customer needs a website developed.

They visit YB TECHKONNECT, create an account, describe their requirements, submit a service request and receive suitable technician recommendations or offers.

A registered web developer receives the request, reviews it and submits a quotation.

The customer chooses the developer.

The work is completed, the customer confirms completion, and the platform records the transaction and applicable commission.

This is the fundamental operation of our business.

### 1.2 Our mission

To make professional technical services accessible, discoverable, trustworthy and affordable by connecting customers with capable service providers through a single digital platform.

### 1.3 Our vision

To become a recognised digital services marketplace in Kenya and eventually expand into other African markets.

### 1.4 Our core objectives

* Help customers find reliable technical professionals.

* Give independent technicians access to customers.

* Create employment and income opportunities.

* Reduce the difficulty of finding technical assistance.

* Establish trust through verification and transparent reviews.

* Provide businesses with convenient access to technical solutions.

* Build a sustainable digital marketplace.

### 2\. THE BUSINESS MODEL

YB TECHKONNECT operates as a two-sided marketplace.

CUSTOMERS

People and organisations requiring services

YB TECHKONNECT

Discovery • Matching • Coordination • Trust • Transactions

SERVICE PROVIDERS

Technicians, developers and professionals

YB TECHKONNECT doesn't necessarily employ every technician. Service providers operate independently under agreed platform terms.

### 2.1 Revenue generation

We will implement several income streams.

| Revenue stream         | How YB earns                                |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Job commissions        | Percentage of completed jobs                |
| Coordination fees      | Fee for arranging a service                 |
| Premium memberships    | Paid technician visibility and benefits     |
| Business subscriptions | Recurring business service packages         |
| Featured listings      | Paid promotional placement                  |
| Maintenance contracts  | Coordination of recurring IT services       |
| Custom solutions       | Development of specialised business systems |

### Example commission model

Let's assume a technician receives a job worth KSh 2,000.

Our illustrative commission is 15%.

| Description        | Amount    |
| ------------------ | --------- |
| Customer payment   | KSh 2,000 |
| Technician's share | KSh 1,700 |
| YB commission      | KSh 300   |

These are proposed figures, not final prices.

We will decide the actual commission structure after researching customer willingness to pay and technician expectations.

### Important payment decision

For the first version, we can allow customers to pay technicians directly and record the agreed commission.

Later, we can introduce integrated payments, potentially through M-Pesa.

The platform must never pretend to hold customer funds or guarantee escrow protection before those capabilities are actually implemented.

### MODULE 07 — Technician Matching and Assignment Engine

This module connects requests to suitable technicians.

Initially, matching will be controlled by the administrator.

Eventually, we can automate it.

### Matching criteria

The system should consider:

* Technician's service category.

* Skills.

* Location.

* Availability.

* Verification status.

* Customer ratings.

* Experience.

* Service mode.

### Example matching logic

A customer requests laptop repair in Kakamega.

The system searches for technicians who:

* Offer computer repair.

* Are available.

* Serve Kakamega.

* Have verified accounts.

* Accept physical jobs.

It then displays suitable providers.

### Future automated matching score

We could use a weighted scoring model:

| Matching factor      | Proposed weight |
| -------------------- | --------------- |
| Skills match         | 30%             |
| Location match       | 20%             |
| Availability         | 15%             |
| Customer rating      | 15%             |
| Experience           | 10%             |
| Response reliability | 10%             |

The total is 100%.

These weights will be configurable and tested against real matching outcomes.

### Outputs

* List of matched technicians.

* Matching score.

* Technician availability.

* Estimated service cost where available.

* Contact or quotation option.

The administrator must retain the ability to override automated recommendations.

### 7.2 How the tables connect

Here is the proposed logical relationship:

```mermaid

erDiagram
    USERS ||--o| CUSTOMER_PROFILES : has
    USERS ||--o| TECHNICIAN_PROFILES : has
    USERS ||--o| BUSINESS_PROFILES : has
    TECHNICIAN_PROFILES ||--o{ TECHNICIAN_SKILLS : has
    SERVICE_CATEGORIES ||--o{ SERVICE_REQUESTS : classifies
    USERS ||--o{ SERVICE_REQUESTS : submits
    SERVICE_REQUESTS ||--o{ REQUEST_ATTACHMENTS : contains
    SERVICE_REQUESTS ||--o{ TECHNICIAN_APPLICATIONS : receives
    TECHNICIAN_PROFILES ||--o{ TECHNICIAN_APPLICATIONS : submits
    TECHNICIAN_APPLICATIONS ||--o{ QUOTATIONS : produces
```

* Database relationships must enforce data integrity.

* Administrative operations must be recorded.
